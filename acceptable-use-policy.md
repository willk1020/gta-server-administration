# Arc Roleplay: Server Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

## 1. Access Requirements
To maintain server integrity and communication standards, all users must meet the following baseline requirements prior to provisioning.
* Active and clear microphone communication.
* Foundational understanding of standard RP terminology and mechanics.
* Commitment to community standards and professional interaction on and off-camera.

## 2. Operational Guidelines & Code of Conduct
* **Roleplay Priority:** All in-server interactions must prioritize roleplay mechanics above all else. Low-quality interactions (e.g., cop-baiting, prioritizing gunplay) are strictly prohibited.
* **Content Restrictions:** NSFW content is strictly forbidden.
* Any mature interactions (18+) must be strictly consensual; violations will result in an immediate and permanent ban.
* **Dispute Resolution:** In-game conflicts must remain within the server environment.
* Out-of-Character (OOC) disputes should be escalated via Discord ticketing rather than disrupting live server
* interactions or content creators' streams.

## 3. Server Mechanics & Exploit Prevention
* **Restricted Action Zones (Greenzones):** Hostile interactions are disabled in designated safe zones, including Hospitals, Player-Owned Businesses, City Hall, and driving schools.
* Fleeing into a Greenzone to evade an active scenario is an exploit; pursuing parties are authorized to continue the interaction inside the zone.
* **Combat Logging & State Evasion:** Disconnecting from the server to avoid consequences (Combat Logging) is punishable by a 24-72 hour suspension. 
* **Exploiting State Mechanics:** Users who are "downed" are removed from the active scene and may not utilize local medical commands to revive and re-enter the interaction.
* **Cooldown Timers ("Rule of Heat"):** Major criminal mechanics (e.g., robberies) enforce a hard 30-minute cooldown period between events to prevent server economy manipulation.
* Violations result in a 24-hour suspension.

## 4. Organization Limits
To maintain server load and faction balance, organization limits are hard-capped.
* **Businesses:** Maximum 5 provisioned members.
* **Organizations/Factions:** Maximum 20 provisioned members.
