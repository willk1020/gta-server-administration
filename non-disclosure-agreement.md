# Arc Roleplay: Non-Disclosure & Confidentiality Agreement

## 1. Purpose & Scope
The purpose of this Agreement is to protect the confidential and proprietary information ("Confidential Information") exchanged in connection with the development, administration, 
and operation of the Arc Roleplay server infrastructure.

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information
Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to:
* **Technical Assets:** Software code, algorithms, source code, object code, flow charts, technical specifications, data structures, and design documents.
* **Intellectual Property:** Trade secrets, trademarks, copyrights, or any proprietary rights related to server software and scripts.
* **Business Operations:** Business plans, financial data, customer/player information, and non-public strategies.

## 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party
Administrative and development staff agree to:
* Maintain strict confidentiality of all proprietary information and withhold disclosure to any unauthorized third party.
* Utilize the Confidential Information solely for authorized server operations and development.
* Prohibit the reverse-engineering, decompiling, or disassembling of any software code, scripts, or assets disclosed under this Agreement without explicit written authorization.

## 4. Return of Assets & Termination
Upon termination of administrative access or at the Disclosing Party’s request, all Confidential Information, including any local copies, notes, or downloaded assets, must be immediately returned or destroyed.
