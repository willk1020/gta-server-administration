# Arc Roleplay: Incident Management & Escalation Policy

## 1. Overview
Established structured disciplinary frameworks to manage user violations, ensuring fair, documented, and progressive resolutions across community platforms.

## 2. Progressive Disciplinary Matrix (Communication Channels)
Enforced strict communication and behavior policies within the community Discord. Escalation paths for violations include:
* **Level 1 (Warning):** Verbal or written directive from staff to cease disruptive behavior.
* **Level 2 (Timeout/Mute):** Temporary restriction of communication privileges if warnings are ignored.
* **Level 3 (Review):** Mandatory staff mediation to resolve ongoing issues and determine if further access restrictions are required.

## 3. Organization Strike System & Probation
Managed faction compliance using a documented "Strike" system to prevent larger organizations from exploiting server mechanics or bullying smaller user groups.
* **Strike Accumulation:** Organizations receive strikes for unauthorized territory changes, unapproved rule-of-engagement violations, or exploiting perma-death rules. 
* **Probationary Status:** Accumulating three (3) strikes automatically places the organization on a staff-monitored probationary period.
* **Disbandment:** Any further policy violations during the probationary period result in immediate administrative disbandment of the organization.
