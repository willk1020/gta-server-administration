# Hells Iron Motorcycles: IT Service Management (ITSM) & CRM

## 1. Overview
Designed and operated an independent service business within the live server ecosystem, managing full-cycle client requests, inventory pricing, and customer relationship management. 

## 2. Service Desk & Work Order Management
Created a structured ticketing and billing pipeline to manage high-volume service requests.
* **Work Order Ticketing:** Tracked individual service tickets from intake to completion. Documented itemized invoices for custom requests
* (e.g., Invoice #1 tracking $2,040 in labor and $4,600 in materials for forced induction and paint upgrades).
* **Margin & Profit Tracking:** Maintained dynamic pricing databases for mechanical services and high-tier assets. Calculated base costs, retail pricing, and profit margins
* (e.g., tracking a $8,750 net profit on $33,500 base-cost units) to ensure operational sustainability.
* **Granular Asset Pricing:** Managed standard pricing sheets for individual modifications, including standardized repair costs ($200 per material used) and performance upgrades.

## 3. Client Relationship Management (CRM)
* **Client Database:** Managed an active client roster tracking service histories, package tiers, and vehicle data.
* **Service Level Agreements (SLAs):** Implemented and tracked custom VIP service packages. Successfully managed tiered client profiles
* (e.g., applying automated "Level 2 - 15%" SLA discounts directly to subtotaled repair invoices for enrolled clients).
